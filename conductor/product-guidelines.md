# Product Guidelines

## Technical Prose and Documentation
- **Educational & Detailed:** Documentation should explain the "why" behind orchestration steps, helping users understand model behaviors and the multi-agent deliberation process.
- **Experimental & Informative:** Maintain the project's original spirit of exploration. Documentation should be detailed but not overly formal, reflecting the "vibe code" nature of the prototype.

## Visual Identity and Brand Messaging
- **Thematic "Council" Aesthetic:** Lean heavily into the academic and formal metaphor of a "Council." Use terminology like "Deliberation," "Summoning," "Chamber," and "Verdict."
- **Thematic Icons and Styling:** UI elements should reflect this formal setting (e.g., icons representing gavels, scrolls, or mahogany tables) to provide a unique and memorable user experience.

## Software Quality and Engineering Principles
- **Iterative & Experimental:** Prioritize rapid prototyping and functionality over rigid, enterprise-grade architecture. The goal is to facilitate experimentation and exploration of AI model side-by-side performance.
- **Functional Stability:** While architecture is flexible, the core 3-stage deliberation pipeline must remain reliable and easy to modify for new experiments.
